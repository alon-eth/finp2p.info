How the connections were derived

Rules
  • settles: Universal Blockchain Connector settles every business app's ledger instructions (its listing: "all types of transactions from all other Super-Apps into any ledger").
  • custody: Custodian Service serves every business app's investors; custody technology providers plug in behind it; staking runs on custodied assets.
  • cash: Payment Provider Service gives the cash leg (DvP, PvP, repo) to any app serving payers, traders, collateral desks, investors or issuers. Universal Stablecoin Connector feeds it and any trading or collateral app.
  • messaging: FIX Connector for apps with Trading in their categories and the repo/collateral hubs; ISO 20022 for collateral, CSD, clearing and payment apps.
  • crosschain: LayerZero Connector and Cross-Chain Asset Converter extend the ledger connector and serve issuer tools and collateral apps that move assets between chains.
  • distributes: issuer tools list onto the Asset Distribution and Access Hub ("core infrastructure used by all business SuperApps"), which feeds venues, investor front ends and the CSD platform; asset marketplaces feed investor front ends.
  • matching: Exberry supplies order matching to venue-type apps.
  • collateral: explicit relationships in listings (HQLAx to Intraday Repo, DTCC experiment to Collateral Mobility Manager) plus collateral flowing into lending and pools.
  • data: Kaiko (prices), Particula (risk ratings) and the Social and Market Data SuperApp (sentiment) feed apps that trade, lend, manage collateral or show assets to investors; post-trade apps send data to Ledgible.
  • identity: Prove products verify identities at onboarding (custody, investor front end, issuers) and payees before funds move.

Institutions: a solid line means the app's SuperApps store listing names the organisation as a partner; a dashed line means the attachment comes from a public report or announcement (the GDF and ISDA sandbox reports, Ownera's press, its Open Collateral Network site) rather than the listing. An institution can be a participant, a client or a technology partner; the note on each link says which, and every link carries its source.

Not drawn: the FinP2P Router License, which every app requires. The optional overlay draws a faint dashed line between any two apps that share two or more primary participant types, the store's own signal that they can live on the same Router.
